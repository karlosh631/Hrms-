## 1. Microsoft's massive M365 outage stretches past 12 hours as its fixes keep failing

### Overview

Microsoft’s massive Microsoft 365 outage has stretched past 12 hours.

Initial fixes attempted by the software giant keep failing.

The pervasive incident has damaged Exchange Online and dependent services.

In Australia, organizations are calling the event the "Microsoft long weekend."

The outage has effectively made Friday a write-off for affected workplaces.


### What happened

The outage officially started on Thursday at around 18:00 UTC.

Microsoft 365 users quickly lost the ability to access email.

Teams meetings could no longer be attended during the incident.

Cloud PCs and Copilot became completely unusable for impacted users.

A total of another 15 hosted services suffered disrupted full access.

Services not requiring Exchange Online have remained functional.

Microsoft originally blamed the issue on servers failing automated health checks.

These servers became unavailable due to this mysterious problem.
