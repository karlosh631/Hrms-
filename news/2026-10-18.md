## 1. NY announces game ownership bill requiring ownership rather than licensing

### Overview
New York has joined legislative efforts to protect video game consumers.
Representative Ritchie Torres has introduced the Power To The Players Act.
Torres serves as the representative for New York's 15th congressional district.
The proposed federal legislation targets anti-consumer practices in the gaming market.
