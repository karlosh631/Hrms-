## 1. Anthropic prices IPO at $83 a share, above projected range

### Overview

Anthropic has officially priced its long-awaited initial public offering at $83 per share.

The final price landed above the company's initial projected range of $70 to $78 per share.

The Claude maker expanded its offering, selling 980 million shares to eager market investors.

The sale generated $81.3 billion in total proceeds for the artificial intelligence company.

This landmark transaction surpasses SpaceX’s $75 billion record for the largest IPO in history by proceeds raised.

Anthropic will list its shares on the Nasdaq stock exchange under the ticker symbol ANT.

The IPO signals robust investor appetite for leading artificial intelligence firms.

Demand remained strong despite recent market jitters caused by the departure of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang.

The listing represents a pivotal moment for the public trading of frontier AI developers.

### What happened

Anthropic initially planned to offer 825 million shares before expanding the size of the deal.

At the final pricing of $83 per share, Anthropic is valued at roughly $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

This reflects a major increase from its $965 billion valuation recorded in May.

In May, Anthropic raised $65 billion during its final private funding round.

The final valuation fell short of the $2 trillion market cap the company reportedly targeted several months ago.

The company's seven co-founders each sold nearly 3.4 million shares in the public offering.

Chief Executive Officer Dario Amodei was among the co-founders selling shares in the IPO.

Each co-founder brought in approximately $282 million from their respective share sales.

The financial details were formally detailed in Anthropic's S-1 filing.

Existing venture investors and strategic corporate partners achieved a massive windfall from the pricing.

### Why it matters

The massive fundraising highlights intense public market demand for artificial intelligence market leaders.

It demonstrates investor willingness to back high-profile AI firms despite broader tech market volatility.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s exit had recently shaken confidence across the broader technology industry.

A strong public market entrance validates Anthropic’s positioning against major market competitors.

Key strategic partners hold substantial equity positions that overshadow traditional venture capital firms.

Amazon holds the largest stake among corporate partners, owning nearly 15% of Anthropic.

Google maintains a strategic equity position holding almost 14% of the company.

Major technology companies agreed to purchase an additional $26 billion in non-voting shares alongside the IPO.

This private placement group includes AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC Partners.

Several existing VC backers are planning to purchase billions of dollars more in shares during the IPO.

### Technical details

Anthropic will officially begin trading as a public company on Thursday.

The company will trade publicly on the Nasdaq under the stock ticker ANT.

No single venture capital investor holds more than a 5% stake in the business.

Sequoia holds approximately 1% of the company’s equity.

Former Google CEO Eric Schmidt also holds a stake of roughly 1% in Anthropic.

Singaporean sovereign wealth fund GIC retains an estimated 1% ownership position.

Other backers making billions include Iconiq, Menlo Ventures, Coatue, and Sound Ventures.

Sound Ventures was launched by Guy Oseary alongside actor Ashton Kutcher.

Spark Capital was Anthropic's first institutional investor and led its 2023 Series C round.
