## 1. Pennsylvania will require labels on AI-generated ads

### Overview

Pennsylvania has enacted new legislation requiring disclosures on artificial intelligence advertisements.
Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act into law on Thursday.
The statute targets any advertisement significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence.
Pennsylvania is now one of the first states to require formal disclosures on AI content.
The mandate requires labels to be delivered in the same format as the advertisement itself.
Regulations apply across multiple media formats, including audio, video, still images, and text.
Both multi-billion-dollar corporate campaigns and small local flyers fall within the scope of the bill.
Legal analysts note that the newly enacted statute contains multiple loopholes and ambiguities.

### What happened

On Thursday, Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro officially signed the new advertising act into law.
The legislation is officially titled the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act.
The bill was formally introduced by Pennsylvania State Senator Nick Pisciottano.
Under the new statute, advertisements using artificial intelligence must include an explicit disclosure.
This rule applies to any advertisement that has been significantly modified or generated by AI.
Disclosures must be provided in the identical format as the advertisement itself.
A video commercial must contain a video disclosure under the statutory rules.
An audio advertisement must feature an accompanying audio disclosure.
The law covers high-profile television commercials produced by multi-billion-dollar companies.
The legislation also applies to small restaurants printing crowded, ugly flyers for wing specials.
The office of State Senator Nick Pisciottano did not immediately return a request for comment.

### Why it matters

Pennsylvania is among the very first states to establish legal disclosure rules for AI media.
The requirement applies strictly regardless of where an advertisement was originally produced.
Out-of-state advertisers must comply if their commercials are expected to air in Pennsylvania.
Viewers in other states may consequently see AI disclosure labels on interstate broadcasts.
A commercial broadcast across several states could require disclosures solely due to Pennsylvania airplay.
