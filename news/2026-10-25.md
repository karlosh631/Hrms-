## 1. Pennsylvania will require labels on AI-generated ads

### Overview

Pennsylvania has enacted new legislation requiring disclosures on artificial intelligence advertisements.
Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act into law on Thursday.
The statute targets any advertisement significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence.
Pennsylvania is now one of the first states to require formal disclosures on AI content.
The mandate requires labels to be delivered in the same format as the advertisement itself.
