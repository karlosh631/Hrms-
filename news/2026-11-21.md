## 2. Signal slams door on prying AI agents to protect privacy

### Overview

Signal has updated its iOS and Android apps to block AI agents by default.

The encrypted messaging app is fighting back against prying software.

Apple, Google, and Microsoft face criticism for inadequate AI controls.

Tech giants are called out for failing to protect developers.

Agentic systems are gaining access to private communications.


### What happened

Signal CEO Meredith Whittaker published a blog post detailing the privacy risks.

Google and Apple are deeply integrating agentic capabilities into their operating systems.

Google Gemini Intelligence and Apple Siri AI use onscreen awareness features.

These OS AI agents scan information visible on device screens.

Signal data is captured and made available to agentic systems.

Private messages ingested by these systems may end up on external servers.

Signal updates now block Siri on iOS and Gemini on Android from taking screenshots.

AI agents process screenshots using optical character resolution to extract text.


### Why it matters

Whittaker warns that disabling this protection risks exposing Signal content.

Content sent by contacts can also be exposed to AI agents.

Screenshot blocking is only a partial solution for application privacy.

AI agents may have other ways to access application data through device file systems.

Application developers lack robust tools to prevent AI agents from undermining privacy.

Big tech companies do not provide ways to easily restrict AI data access.

Restrictions often require making drastic compromises to the core user experience.

These limitations currently prevent implementing protections on macOS.


### Technical details

Apple is implementing some protections from AI agents on macOS.

These changes seem designed to defend against third-party access rather than limit Siri.

Siri can be turned on or off separately from other controls.

macOS 27.3 is currently undergoing public beta testing.

The desktop OS gets more granular controls on Device Control and Data Access APIs.

Full Disk Access controls are also being updated in the new OS version.

Users will face more permission prompts for apps seeking data access.

Backup software and assistive technologies will require an entitlement from Apple.


### Industry impact

Apple stated that risks associated with AI access will grow substantially.

Apple is reportedly working on its own AI agent to compete with Anthropic and OpenAI.

Signal's decision to block Siri shows AI agents complicate trust decisions.

AI agents often spirit data away to the cloud.

Merchants and consumers face disrupted business relationships due to third-party agents.

Amazon has blocked shopping agents like Meta Muse from its e-commerce site.

Walmart has invited third-party agents to interact through its AI Gateway.

Anthropic has blocked subscription customers from using third-party harnesses.


### What to watch next

United Airlines has banned transactions from unauthorized AI bots.

Target, Home Depot, Etsy, and eBay have erected similar barriers.

Financial institutions like JPMorgan Chase and Citigroup are limiting third-party agents.

A burgeoning industry in bot detection and mitigation is led by Cloudflare and Akamai.

Web browsers were once called user agents based on idealistic notions of service.

Online advertising and data collection have muddied that original vision.

AI agents now face similar scrutiny over who they truly serve.

The backlash has arrived after years of agents demanding digital data access.

## 4. VMware patches vCenter zero-day exploited in attacks since September

### Overview

Broadcom has released security updates to fix a critical VMware vCenter Server zero-day vulnerability.
The authentication bypass flaw is tracked under the identifier CVE-2026-123890.
Chinese-linked hackers have actively exploited this vulnerability in zero-day attacks since September 2026.
The issue affects the VMware Directory Service located inside vCenter Server.
vCenter Server is a management platform organizations use for managing virtualized environments.
The vulnerability affects vCenter regardless of whether Enhanced Linked Mode is enabled.

### What happened

Broadcom patched the vulnerability on November 19 via security advisory VMSA-2026-0009.
The advisory also fixes four additional vulnerabilities affecting VMware SDDC Manager and VMware Tools for Windows.
