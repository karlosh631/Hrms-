## 1. FAA lifts nationwide ground stop that blocked takeoffs for hours

### Overview

A communications problem shut down takeoffs across the US on Friday afternoon.

The national ground stop lasted for more than four hours before being lifted.

The FAA issued the nationwide ground stop at 3:44PM ET.

The stop was enacted due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety.

Officials announced the ground stop was lifted at 8:15PM ET, allowing flights to resume.
