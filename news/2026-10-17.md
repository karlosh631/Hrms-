## 1. Replit is no longer working with Anthropic, CEO says

### Overview

Replit has reportedly parted ways with artificial intelligence research lab Anthropic.

The announcement was made by Replit CEO and co-founder Amjad Masad.

Masad delivered the remarks onstage at TechCrunch Disrupt on Thursday.

The coding platform previously used Anthropic's Claude models to power its tools.

Masad stated that the decision was driven by cost, performance, and partnership factors.

However, insiders familiar with the relationship rejected Masad's statements.

Sources claim that the commercial partnership with Anthropic remains in place.

### What happened

Amjad Masad announced the split during an onstage interview at TechCrunch Disrupt.

Masad told the audience that he believes ChatGPT is better now.

Replit had previously announced a partnership with OpenAI in August.

That August announcement did not indicate an exit from Anthropic at the time.

Masad explained that Replit evaluated models using internal benchmarks.

He noted that public benchmarks were also published by the company.

Masad stated that OpenAI was simply a better partner for now.

A person familiar with the partnership disputed Masad's public statement.

Speaking anonymously, the insider insisted that the partnership is still active.

TechCrunch reached out to Replit seeking formal clarification on the matter.

### Why it matters

Replit is an AI coding platform that has become a favorite among startup founders.

The platform is also popular with independent software developers.

Working with Anthropic had previously helped Replit grow its revenue significantly.

Replit's business model is shifting toward larger enterprise customers.

Masad said enterprise deals made up only about 2% of revenue last year.

Enterprise contracts have surged to roughly 15% of company revenue this year.

More than 50% of Replit's total revenue now comes from B2B customers.

Replit is not currently sharing its exact annual recurring revenue numbers.

### Technical details

Replit offers a cloud-based software development platform.

The service lets users develop and deploy applications directly inside web browsers.

Replit uses internal benchmarks to assess key performance metrics for AI models.

Masad said Sol and Astra perform better at building vibe-coded applications from scratch.

Masad also outlined technical requirements for deploying AI agents safely.

He argued that AI agents require a well-constructed digital habitat to work properly.

He compared this agent habitat to an office space built for human workers.

Essential technical elements include robust orchestration and automated error recovery.

Safe deployment environments require strong security protocols and isolated sandboxing.

Agent environments also rely on dependable networking systems.

Proper identity management and authorization controls are necessary before deploying AI agents safely.

### Industry impact

Evidence shows Anthropic models were being utilized by Replit until very recently.

Traces on Replit's website and social media show active Anthropic usage weeks ago.

A late September blog post referenced Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.

That same post cited Claude Fable 5.1 in Replit Agent's production environment.

A Replit product employee recently posted about Anthropic on the social platform X.

The employee promoted the capabilities of Anthropic's flagship model Opus 5.5.

Shifting between model providers highlights fierce competition across AI coding platforms.

AI platforms are continuously balancing model inferencing expenses against raw coding capability.

Enterprise adoption requires high-performing models to support automated coding workflows.

### What to watch next

Observers are waiting to see if Replit officially responds to requests for comment.

TechCrunch stated it will update its reporting if Replit provides clarification.

It remains unclear how Replit will resolve contradictory reports about its Anthropic contract.

Developers will watch for changes in Replit Agent's underlying model infrastructure.

Future updates will show whether Claude models remain accessible within the platform.

Analysts will track whether Replit's enterprise revenue continues its rapid growth trajectory.
