## 1. Replit is no longer working with Anthropic, CEO says

### Overview

Replit has reportedly parted ways with artificial intelligence research lab Anthropic.

The announcement was made by Replit CEO and co-founder Amjad Masad.

Masad delivered the remarks onstage at TechCrunch Disrupt on Thursday.

The coding platform previously used Anthropic's Claude models to power its tools.

Masad stated that the decision was driven by cost, performance, and partnership factors.

However, insiders familiar with the relationship rejected Masad's statements.
