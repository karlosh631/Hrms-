## 1. PC chip plunge wipes out a quarter of Intel’s value

### Overview

Intel suffered a steep stock collapse after releasing its latest quarterly results.
The company saw its share price plunge by nearly twenty-four percent.
A sudden downturn in personal computer processor sales sparked investor panic.
The sell-off erased nearly a quarter of Intel's total market valuation.

CEO Lip Bu Tan warned that the PC processor market faces prolonged challenges.
Overall revenue reached sixteen point five billion dollars despite the PC slump.
